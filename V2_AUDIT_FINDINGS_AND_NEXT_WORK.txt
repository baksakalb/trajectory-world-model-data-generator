Problem A: prescribed post-throw movement is not reliably realized
------------------------------------------------------------------
Failed examples:
- seed 920013 / R14-medium / post_throw_backward: no meaningful backward motion.
- seed 920014 / R15-long / post_throw_strafe_left: no meaningful left strafe;
  a tiny look is followed by generic random continuation.
- seed 920016 / R17-short / post_throw_diagonal: essentially stationary before
  generic camera/random continuation.
- seed 920015 / R16-very_long / post_throw_strafe_right is the positive control:
  its right strafe is visible and understandable.

Required fix:
- Measure realized player displacement in the prescribed local/world direction,
  not merely the presence of W/A/S/D bits in metadata.
- Begin the prescribed movement immediately after the throw/Q-retention window
  and sustain it for a human-readable interval (normally at least 1.0 second),
  unless collision escape temporarily takes priority.
- After escape, resume the remaining prescribed movement budget.
- Add per-profile signed-displacement validators and focused regression videos.

Problem B: meaningful actions are sometimes only a few frames long
------------------------------------------------------------------
Failed examples:
- seed 920047 / south wall glance-left low / wall-then-floor.
- seed 920048 / south wall glance-right high / adjacent-corner.
The glances last less than approximately one second and do not resemble a human
glance. The same concern applies generally to backward movement, strafing,
reorientation, and scene-establishing actions that currently occur for only a
few frames.

Required fix:
- Define minimum semantic dwell times in seconds/observation steps for glance,
  look-away, reacquisition, post-throw movement, and scene establishment.
- Validate contiguous realized duration, not just the existence of an action edge.
- Preserve smooth transitions, but do not allow smoothing to consume nearly the
  entire intended action.

Ground-only/context-free examples:

These start by looking at the ground, so the viewer has no prior evidence that

Required fix:

  view containing its target/boundary and useful arena context near eye level.

  observation and should not dominate the event.
- Floor-first missions must establish both the relevant floor region and the
  later wall/object/ramp/hoop when that later target is part of the outcome.
- Add visibility/framing metadata and validators: target visible before preview,
  useful world-pixel fraction, pitch bounds during establishment, and no long
  sky-only/ground-only runs.

Problem D: required grenade outcomes are not kept visible
---------------------------------------------------------
Examples 920062, 920067, 920072, 920074, 920077, and 920079 do not reliably show
the required settle, bounce, multi-bounce, or roll. A high arc itself is fine,
but the camera must reacquire the landing region and keep the grenade or expected
contact region visible around the semantic event. The camera need not perfectly
track the grenade; it must provide understandable evidence of the result.

Required fix:
- Introduce event-aware camera phases: establish scene, aim/preview, follow
  approximate flight/landing region, hold through required contacts, then active
  continuation.
- Record/project grenade and target visibility around first contact, each required
  bounce, hoop crossing, and rest. Reject missions missing adequate visibility.

Problem E: direct-settle currently overrides plausible simulator physics
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Failed examples:
- 920058 floor S0 near/low direct-settle.
- 920066 floor S2 far/medium direct-settle.
- 920070 floor S3 near/low direct-settle.
- 920078 floor S5 far/medium direct-settle.
- 920082 floor S6 near/low direct-settle.
The trajectory appears to predict a rebound, but the live grenade sticks to the
floor. This looks inconsistent with the otherwise good simulator.

Confirmed code cause:
When a floor_bounce_rest recipe has outcome direct_settle, SpawnGrenade currently
overrides its simulation configuration with BounceRestitution=0.0,
TangentialDamping=1.0, and MaxBounces=1. This is an artificial data-generation
override, not ordinary physical behavior. The validator then accepts BounceCount
<=1 and less than 100 cm post-contact travel. Preview parity is currently written
as true without validating bounce/rest parity, so structural validation missed it.

Required fix:
- Remove the zero-restitution/full-damping forced stick behavior.
- Redefine or replace direct_settle with a physically plausible low-energy short
  rebound/short-settle outcome derived from the canonical simulator parameters.
- Compute preview-to-realized parity from actual contact count, bounce count,
  timing, landing region, and rest behavior; never write it as an unconditional
  true value.
- Keep one-bounce, multi-bounce, and long-roll outcomes distinct and measurable.

Problem F: invalid mission outcomes can leave the arena
-------------------------------------------------------
- 920060 S0 far/high multi-bounce appears to leave the map and does not visibly
  realize the required multiple bounces. This must not receive mission credit.
- Out-of-bounds grenades are acceptable during random play and the future long
  play because they may arise naturally.
- Out-of-bounds is forbidden for every ordinary object, wall, floor, ramp, hoop,
  or sequence step unless that step explicitly requests the new out-of-bounds
  mission.

New explicit out-of-bounds mission
----------------------------------
Add a small, intentional mission family/cell set for throwing beyond the arena.
The mission must:
1. Establish the relevant boundary and surrounding arena at roughly eye level.
2. Hold that context for a human-readable interval.
3. Acquire a trajectory that clearly crosses the selected boundary.
4. Throw and record the authoritative boundary exit event/direction/frame.
5. Keep the exit direction visible long enough to understand what happened.
6. Finish with a short active continuation.
Credit requires a deliberate exit through the selected boundary. Merely losing
the grenade, missing another target, or leaving through a different boundary is
failure. Random/long-play exits remain valid natural play but are not credited as
this mission unless explicitly scheduled.

Problem G: lateral ramp cross-over can be semantically unclear
--------------------------------------------------------------
- seed 920106 / lateral left-to-right lower cross-over passed structural checks,
  but the reviewer could not tell what it did.
Required fix: establish both sides of the ramp, keep the crossing corridor in
frame, and validate a geometric side-to-side crossing rather than only absence of
ramp contact. A credited label must be understandable from the stored frames.

Long-play dataset proposal
--------------------------
Add long play as a third data source after the above V2 defects are corrected and
requalified. Tentative overall allocation: V1 plus V2 together form about two
thirds of the final observations; long play forms about one third. Do not freeze
that percentage until duration/quality calibration is measured.

Long play should combine movement and grenade behavior coherently for extended
connected trajectories: throw, release, relocate, survey, reacquire, throw again,
camera-only Q preview/cancel/reacquire, cooldown recovery, and persistent multiple
grenades. It has no prescribed object target on every throw. Natural contacts,
bounces, rolls, and out-of-bounds throws are allowed.

Long-play behavioral priors:
- Eye-level and central-map viewing should be the dominant camera state.
- Looking sharply up/down and inspecting walls should occur, but less frequently
  and for purposeful bounded intervals.
- Prefer traversing/revisiting the arena interior over repeatedly approaching walls.
- Detect contact/stall quickly, execute Q-free escape, confirm displacement away
  from the obstacle, and resume coherent play.
- Use human-readable action dwell times and smooth transitions.
- Preserve grenades in the world so later observations contain persistent resting
  and flying grenades; bound counts only for performance/safety.
- Avoid long idle/fixed-input tails. Long duration must mean connected useful
  behavior, not padding.
- Store segment/event metadata so the mixture of movement, previews, throws,
  relocations, reacquisitions, contacts, escapes, persistence, and exits can be
  reconstructed and validated.
